## What is N8N?

[N8N](https://n8n.io/) is a powerful, open-source workflow automation tool that lets you connect your favorite apps and services without writing code. Think of it as the bridge between all your business tools—from Gmail and Slack to Shopify, Google Sheets, and your CRM.

Unlike expensive enterprise automation platforms, N8N can be self-hosted (keeping your data private) or used in the cloud, making it perfect for small businesses that need flexibility and cost control.
